02 Roll-forming

From steel coil to finished sheet, in one pass.

Painted steel coil feeds the roll-former and comes out as a profiled sheet, cut at the length you order. Long sheets mean fewer joints on the roof — and fewer places for water to get in.

03 The range

Eight profiles. One factory.

Scroll through the range — the sheet on screen reshapes itself into each profile we roll.

C-868

Deep Roman tile

Our boldest tile profile: tall, rounded barrels that give a roof the look of classic clay tiles, pressed into one steel sheet.

C-Wave

Flowing wave tile

A softer S-shaped wave with stepped courses — the profile on many of the maroon and red roofs we have supplied.

C-800

Classic Roman tile

Rounded barrels at a tighter pitch than C-868 for a finer, more even tiled look across the whole roof.

C-840

Flat-step tile

Crisp, low ribs and pronounced steps. It reads modern and architectural, especially in charcoal.

Corrugated

The everyday workhorse

Even sine-wave corrugations. A cost-effective, proven choice for homes, stores, farm structures and extensions.

Box Profile · IT-5

Strong trapezoidal ribs

Five deep, square ribs per sheet for stiffness over wider spans — the go-to for commercial, industrial and modern residential roofs.

Stone-coated · Roman

Granule-coated Roman tile

Steel tile panels with narrow Roman barrels and a stone-chip finish: the matte, textured look on many of the homes we have roofed.

Stone-coated · Shake

Textured shake tile

Offset shake blocks under a stone-granule coating. Matte and deeply textured, it hides fixings and softens rain noise.
